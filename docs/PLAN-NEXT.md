# Stress test: the plan for review items 5–9

*2026-10-12. Each item was tested against its value hypothesis, its failure modes, the spec's non-negotiables, cost, and real data where possible. Verdicts: **build** (as refined here), **build small**, or **defer** (with the evidence).*

| # | Item | Hypothesis | What the stress test found | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Fit-based openers and a ready-to-send draft | Most outreach cards have no news hook (5 of 9 strong-fit/Warm VCs have news score 0–2), so Vikram needs an opener from what makes the VC a fit | **Value holds.** The research `fit_reason` is third-person analyst notes with caveats ("deal credits remain firm-level", "Xindus stage conflict…"), so pasting it isn't an opener. **Risks and fixes:** (a) misattribution, i.e. crediting a firm deal to the partner personally → the prompt says "your firm" unless the research says personally; (b) invented names or numbers → the same verbatim guard as hooks, checked against the research text + VC name + firm; (c) caveats leaking into the opener → the prompt uses only positive, verified facts and never mentions caveats; (d) latency: Gemini takes 13–70 s per call, too slow on click → **precompute during runs** for outreach and queue VCs, batched, cached by a hash of the research text and settings; (e) the spec forbids AI claims about the founder's company → the pitch line is **Vikram's own text** from Settings, and the draft is assembled in code; (f) no email addresses in the research → Copy only | **Build** |
| 6 | Focus strip and Monday digest | A 10-second answer needs one line: "today: N follow-ups, M emails" | **Strip:** duplicating cards adds scroll and no new information → build a **one-line summary bar** with names that jump to the cards. **Digest:** there's no delivery channel (no email provider in the stack; adding one is a new dependency and account; Slack needs a webhook only the owner can create). Building unused delivery plumbing is waste → build a **digest generator** (`GET /api/digest`, bearer) plus **optional** posting to `DIGEST_WEBHOOK_URL` after each cron run; dormant until the owner sets the URL | **Build small** |
| 7 | Logistics deal discovery | Market funding news would supply "why now" hooks | **Disproved on live data.** (1) 194 recent logistics-funding headlines from 3 Google News queries: 6 name a tracked firm, **0** of those are logistics deals. (2) 35 per-firm "firm + logistics" queries over 90 days: 4 hits, 3 namesakes (Stellaris video game, Lightspeed satellites, Blume Metal), 1 real (Caret/Yantra Packs, already in research). (3) Inc42 and YourStory feeds hold about 3 days of items, 0 hits; Entrackr and VCCircle feeds return 404. Free sources don't yield the signal; per-VC Google News and Tavily already catch firm-named items | **Defer** until a structured funding source (e.g. a paid deals API) is available |
| 8 | Outcome learning | Reply and meeting rates should recalibrate the ranking | **Statistical recalibration isn't feasible yet:** at most 50 VCs and a few weeks give too few outcomes for any threshold change to beat noise. **Logging is cheap and is the prerequisite.** Risk: status changes made through the edit form would bypass quick-action logging → log in both paths. Build an **event log** (`outreach_events`: VC, event, at, fit label, had a hook, action at the time) and an **outreach funnel** on `/quality` (emailed → replied → meeting, by fit label and by hook vs no hook). Recalibrate later once there are ≥ 20 outcomes | **Build small** |
| 9 | Research refresh | Roles and funds drift | Research is **1 day old**. Automated refresh needs web lookups (Tavily credits) and AI extraction that could **overwrite curated, human-verified research**, a data-integrity risk with no current benefit. Safeguards already exist: the "Re-verify role" badge (180 days) and the "Possible move" badge from role-change hints | **Defer** (revisit at 90 days) |

## Cross-cutting checks

- **Spec non-negotiables:** openers are AI text grounded in research with a code guard; the AI still produces no URLs, dates, scores or actions. Drafts are assembled in code from the opener plus Vikram's own pitch line. Nothing is written to `vcs` beyond `apply_signals` / `update_my_fields`; drafts and events live in `vc_profile` and a new table.
- **Cost:** item 5 adds about 1–2 Gemini calls per run (only VCs whose research or settings changed); items 6 and 8 add none. No Tavily.
- **Failure isolation:** if opener generation fails, the card falls back to the research talking point; it never blocks scoring or writes.
- **Tests:** opener guard and assembly, digest content, funnel maths, event logging on both edit paths.

## Build order

**5 → 8 → 6**: drafts first (most daily value), then the event log (data starts accumulating), then the summary bar and digest.
